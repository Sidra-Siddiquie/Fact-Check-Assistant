# Fact Check Assistant — M2 Test Inputs and Outputs

## TC02 — Contradicted Claim
**Claim:** The Earth takes 100 days to complete one revolution around the Sun.

**Evidence:** Earth orbits the Sun in an elliptical orbit, completing one revolution in approximately 365.25 days.

**Observed Verdict:** FALSE

**Observation:** The system identified the claim as FALSE because the supplied evidence states approximately 365.25 days, not 100 days.

## TC03 — Partially Supported Claim
**Claim:** The Earth revolves around the Sun and completes one revolution in 100 days.

**Evidence:** Earth revolves around the Sun in an elliptical orbit and completes one revolution in approximately 365.25 days.

**Observed Verdict:** PARTIALLY TRUE

**Observation:** The system recognised that Earth revolves around the Sun but identified the 100-day duration as incorrect.

## TC04 — Insufficient Evidence
**Claim:** The Earth has exactly 10,000 mountains.

**Evidence:** The Earth revolves around the Sun in an elliptical orbit and completes one revolution in approximately 365.25 days.

**Observed Verdict:** UNVERIFIED

**Observation:** The supplied evidence did not contain information about the number of mountains on Earth.

## TC05 — Conflicting Evidence
**Claim:** The Earth completes one revolution around the Sun in 365 days.

**Evidence:** Evidence 1 states approximately 365 days. Evidence 2 states approximately 400 days.

**Observed Verdict:** UNVERIFIED

**Observation:** The system identified conflicting information and explained the uncertainty.

## TC10 — Compound Claim
**Claim:** The Earth revolves around the Sun and the Moon revolves around the Earth.

**Evidence:** The Earth orbits the Sun in an elliptical orbit. The Moon orbits the Earth.

**Observed Verdict:** TRUE

**Observation:** The system examined both statements separately and found both supported by the supplied evidence.
