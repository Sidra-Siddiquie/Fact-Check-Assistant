\- Added V1, V2 and V3 prompt selection and generation.

\### Prompt Engineering

\### Testing

\- Conducted 10 manual test scenarios.

\- Tested supported, contradicted, partially supported, insufficient and conflicting evidence.

\- Tested empty inputs, long claims, missing sources and compound claims.

\- Recorded the observed results in test-data/M2\_Test\_Results.txt.

\- Saved additional test examples in test-data/M2\_Test\_Inputs\_Outputs.txt.



\### Documentation

\- Updated project documentation.

\- Added test evidence and the final prompt.

\- Continued preparation of Milestone 2 documentation.



\### Known Limitations

\- AI responses are generated through a manual ChatGPT workflow.

\- Evidence retrieval and independent source verification are not automated.

\- History and test results are stored in the current Streamlit session.
